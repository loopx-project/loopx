## Disposition and current design

[中文版](personal-follow-through-v0.zh-CN.md) is the semantic mirror of this file.
The proposed independent lifecycle and M1–M3 plan are superseded by the accepted
[manager RFC](capable-manager-semantic-handoff-v0.md), especially §5.2/§5.3/§5.11/§5.12
and its acceptance and dependency plan. This is a design consolidation; it does not
claim implementation, deployment, account authorization or completed acceptance.

The retained [Lark personal follow-through profile](../../product/use-cases/office-operations/personal-follow-through.md)
contains source interpretation, verified responsibility, personal privacy scope,
TypeScript/Node implementation constraints, frozen semantic evaluation and the
attention-cost pilot. Its mapping to manager A3/A5–A10/A13–A15/A20 and M1–M4
replaces the former independent delivery plan. Shared state, grants, recovery and
return stay with their existing owners. No parallel task/proposal store or scheduler
is approved. Future engineering and evidence follow those canonical owners.
