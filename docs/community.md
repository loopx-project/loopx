## Report, Request, Or Contribute

Choose the narrowest issue form for your public-safe report:

- [Bug report](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
  for reproducible incorrect or unexpected behavior.
- [Feature request](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
  for a concrete product or documentation improvement.
- [First-run feedback](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx/issues/new?template=first_run.yml)
  for optional feedback after installing and running LoopX once.
- [Show and Tell / Adoption](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx/issues/new?template=show_and_tell.yml)
  for a public-safe workflow, result, integration, or adoption story.
- [Usage story](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx/issues/new?template=usage_story.yml)
  for a public report from a LoopX run lasting hours, days, or longer.
- [Contributor task](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx/issues/new?template=contributor-task.yml)
  to claim, propose, or discuss repository-safe work.

Before opening a contribution, read [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
[public/private boundary](public-private-boundary.md). Never publish
credentials, private project context, raw agent sessions, internal links, or
local runtime state.

## Share A Showcase

Start with the [Showcase catalog](showcases/README.md), then use one of these
prompts:

- [Show and Tell / Adoption](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx/issues/new?template=show_and_tell.yml):
  describe what really ran, how LoopX was used, the observed outcome and public
  evidence, remaining limitations, and reproducible artifacts when available.
- [Usage story](https://github.com/loopx-project/loopx/issues/new?template=usage_story.yml):
  record the run duration, capabilities exercised, public-safe recovery story,
  and the outcome LoopX helped complete.

Showcase entries keep evidence boundaries explicit. A report may be useful
before it is independently reproduced, but its status must say what remains
user-reported, assisted, experimental, or unverified.

## Explore The Ecosystem

- [Ecosystem adoption and derivatives catalog](community/ecosystem-adoption.md):
  maintainer-curated public evidence about integrations, mechanism reuse,
  proposals, learning resources, and derivatives.
- [ADOPTERS](../ADOPTERS.md): voluntary, self-attested adoption entries from
  projects and users.

These are different evidence surfaces. A catalog entry records observable
public evidence; it does not imply endorsement or production deployment.
